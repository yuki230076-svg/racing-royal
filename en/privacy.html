Privacy Policy

This page explains what information this game handles and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

  • Play records (anonymous): about 20 kinds of events, such as tutorial progress, races, shop views and purchases, and Scout (Google Firebase Analytics). We do not collect names, email addresses or contact details
  • Gem and Coin balances: paid and free Gem and Coin balances, 1 per 1 day (to track prepaid payment instrument balances under Japan's Payment Services Act. Not sent if you turn off play records)
  • Operator servers: the current version does not use any servers run by the operator (save data and purchase records stay only on your device, in your iCloud, and with Apple and RevenueCat). Before we start any feature that sends information to a server, such as competing against other players' records, we will announce it in the app and revise this policy. To verify purchases, we share a purchaser ID issued by the game (random letters and numbers) with RevenueCat
  • Crash records: details of app crashes and on-screen exceptions, plus your device model and OS version (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Ads: ad display and reward videos (Google AdMob). The advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow it through ATT (App Tracking Transparency)
  • Purchases: purchase records, transaction IDs and membership expiry dates (Apple, RevenueCat). Your card details never reach this game
  • Game Center: leaderboard records and Game Center saves (iCloud Drive)
  • Save data: stored on your device and in your iCloud (key-value storage and Game Center saves). The operator cannot read it

2. How We Use It

To fix bugs, tune playability and difficulty, deliver purchases correctly, and show ads. We do not sell information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

Google (Firebase, AdMob), RevenueCat, and Apple (App Store, iCloud, Game Center). Information is handled under each company's privacy policy.

4. How to Opt Out

Ad tracking: iPhone "Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking". Sending records: turn off "Send play records" in the game's Settings. To request access to or deletion of your data, contact us at the address below. Records needed to verify payments and refunds, and transaction records required by law, are kept for as long as those purposes require.
